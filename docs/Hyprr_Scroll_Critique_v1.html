Scroll section critique

A post-build review of the pinned #system section against the shipped code, not the screenshot alone. The spec was implemented faithfully — dwell, engine colours, the promoted H2, the carousel, the rail buttons are all there. Three specific mechanisms are undoing the work.

SystemScroll.tsx · 30.4 KB Viewport ~1875×852 State 01 / Build No code changed
Overall impression

The build is 85% right and reads as 40% right, because three mechanisms are cancelling out everything that was done well.

A blanket opacity: 0.62 drops the three inactive panels to 2.58:1 — they are not “dimmed”, they are unreadable. A fixed-height composition sits as an island in a 100 vh pane, so on a wide-short laptop roughly 55% of the section is empty. And the skip link floats mid-right with nothing to anchor it. None of these are design decisions that failed — they are three implementation details, each about five lines.

The developer did not miss the brief. Every item below is a finishing problem — the kind that only appears once the structure is right, which is why it could not have been caught earlier.

2The root cause: a fixed-height island in a fluid pane

One structural mistake explains most of what looks wrong.

// SystemScroll.tsx — the pane is 100vh, but its contents are capped
copywrap.style.minHeight = "clamp(240px,38vh,460px)";   // copy column
grid:      height:        "clamp(260px,50vh,600px)";   // panel grid
grid:      margin:        "18px 0";

The pinned pane is height: 100vh. On a 852 px-tall viewport the section fills about 774 px below the header. The content inside caps at 50 vh ≈ 426 px for the grid, and the H2 cluster adds roughly 165 px. That leaves ~180 px of dead space, split above and below by the justifyContent: center on [data-mid] — which is exactly the two voids in the screenshot.

The fix is not “add more padding” — it is to stop capping. The grid and the copy column should be flex: 1; min-height: 0 inside the pane so the composition grows to fill whatever height it is given, with the H2 pinned top and the rail pinned bottom. Panels then scale with the viewport instead of floating in it, which also makes them read as the primary object rather than as a thumbnail.

3Findings

#FindingSeverityRecommendation
S1Blanket opacity makes three panels unreadable
opacity: (0.62 + 0.38 * max(act,conv))		P0Opacity on a container multiplies both its background and its text against the page. Inactive panel text lands at 2.58:1, its border at 1.32:1. Never de-emphasise with container opacity. Hold panels at opacity: 1 and de-emphasise by interpolating the text colour only — which the paint loop already does via mix(MUTE, INK, act). Set inactive text to #C7C3D2 (8.86:1) and let the background tint carry the state.
S2Fixed-height island
50 vh grid in a 100 vh pane		P0Section 2. Replace both caps with flex: 1; min-height: 0; keep a min-height floor only for very short viewports.
S3Rail not visible in the frameP0The rail is the only thing that tells a visitor there are four states and that they can jump. It is display:none in SSR and only enabled in pinned. It must be bottom-anchored inside the pane and visible from the first frame of the pin — not below the fold, not after hydration.
S4Skip link is orphaned
alignItems: flex-end on the H2 row		P1It baseline-aligns to the bottom of a two-line H2, so it floats in mid-air on the right with nothing beside it. Move it to the rail row, right-aligned opposite the ticks. That is where a user looks for section navigation, and it stops it competing with the heading.
S5Rail ticks compound opacity again
opacity: .55 + #8E89A0		P1Same mistake as S1 at smaller scale. Upcoming ticks land near 2.9:1. Drop the opacity; distinguish by colour and weight only — #FFFFFF 600 for current, #C7C3D2 500 for visited, #8E89A0 500 for upcoming.
S6Copy column is the narrower half
.86fr 1.14fr		P1The words explaining the state get less room than the illustration of it. With a two-column chip list inside, the copy reads as sparse fragments. Go to 1fr 1.1fr and force the chip list to a single column below 1200 px so it reads as a list, not a scattered grid.
S7Section has no SSR heightP1<section> renders auto-height, then applyMode() sets 320vh after hydration. That is a large layout shift on a section high in the page. Set minHeight: "320vh" in the inline style for the pinned breakpoint via CSS, and let JS only correct it.
S8Hardcoded greys bypass the tokens
rgb(127 127 127 / .25)		P2The artRow divider and several literals sit outside the token system that was just installed. Route them through --color-line-on-ink. Small, but this is how a fresh token system rots in the first month.
S9Grey strip under the headerP2A ~24 px light band sits between the header and the ink field. It reads as a rendering artefact. Check the preceding section’s bottom padding and the announcement bar — the ink section should butt straight against whatever is above it.

5The fix prompt

Copy this whole block to the coding agent. It is scoped so the working parts cannot be regressed.

Fix the pinned #system section in components/home/SystemScroll.tsx.
Do NOT change: any copy string, the four states, the dwell() remap, the three
modes (pinned/carousel/stacked), the SSR/no-JS baseline, or any other file
except globals.css where noted.

CONTEXT
The section is structurally correct but three mechanisms undo it: blanket
container opacity destroys contrast, fixed-height content leaves the pinned
pane half empty, and the rail/skip link are misplaced.

FIX 1 — Remove all container opacity (P0)
- In paint(), delete `el.style.opacity = (0.62 + 0.38 * Math.max(act, conv))`
  on [data-panel]. Panels stay at opacity 1 always.
- De-emphasise inactive panels by colour only. Set the inactive text colour to
  #C7C3D2 (8.86:1) and interpolate toward #17171A as `act` rises — the existing
  `mix(MUTE, INK, act)` call does this; just change MUTE from #8E89A0 to #C7C3D2.
- Keep the background interpolation (INACTIVE -> BANDS[n]) exactly as is.
- Raise the inactive panel background from rgb(255 255 255 / .06) to
  rgb(255 255 255 / .05) and the border from /.16 to /.22 so the edge reads
  at 3:1 without opacity.
- In the rail ticks, delete `el.style.opacity`. Distinguish by colour + weight:
  current #FFFFFF weight 600, visited #C7C3D2 weight 500, upcoming #8E89A0
  weight 500.
- Replace the hardcoded `rgb(127 127 127 / .25)` in artRow with
  `var(--color-line-on-ink)`.

FIX 2 — Make the composition fill the pane (P0)
The pane is 100vh but the content caps at 50vh, leaving ~180px of dead space
split above and below. Restructure the pinned pane as a three-row flex column:
  row 1  H2 block            flex: none
  row 2  stage (copy + grid) flex: 1, min-height: 0
  row 3  rail                flex: none
- Remove `copywrap.style.minHeight = "clamp(240px,38vh,460px)"`.
  Use `height: 100%` with `min-height: 0`.
- Remove the grid's `height: "clamp(260px,50vh,600px)"` and `margin: "18px 0"`.
  Use `height: 100%; min-height: 0`.
- Keep a single floor on the stage: `min-height: 320px` for very short viewports.
- Remove `justifyContent: "center"` from [data-mid]; the flex rows now do the work.
- Panels must scale with the pane. Verify at 1440x900, 1920x1080 and 1280x720.

FIX 3 — Reposition the skip link (P1)
Move the "Skip to commerce paths ↓" anchor out of the H2 flex row and into the
rail row, right-aligned opposite the four ticks. Remove `alignItems: "flex-end"`
from the H2 row (the H2 is then the only child and needs no alignment).

FIX 4 — Rail always visible in pinned mode (P1)
The rail must be visible from the first frame of the pin, bottom-anchored inside
the 100vh pane, never below the fold. Confirm applyMode() sets it to
display:block before first paint, not after.

FIX 5 — Column ratio and chip list (P1)
- Change the stage from `.86fr 1.14fr` to `1fr 1.1fr`.
- The state chip list currently uses repeat(auto-fit, minmax(170px,1fr)) which
  gives a scattered 2-column grid. Below 1200px force a single column.

FIX 6 — Stop the hydration layout shift (P1)
The section renders auto-height then jumps to 320vh after hydration. Add a CSS
media rule (not inline JS) so the pinned height applies before hydration:
  @media (min-width:901px) and (prefers-reduced-motion:no-preference) {
    #system { min-height: 320vh; }
  }
Let applyMode() only correct it, never establish it.

FIX 7 — Focus rings on dark (P1)
The global :focus-visible uses --color-link #6947FF, which is 2.4:1 on ink.
Add a rule so any focusable element inside a .on-ink section (or #system)
uses --color-link-on-ink #A38DFF.

FIX 8 — Visual slots (do now, fill later)
Add empty, styled, aspect-ratio-reserved containers so imagery can drop in
without a relayout. Each gets width+height attributes or aspect-ratio to
prevent CLS, and renders a neutral ink-tinted placeholder until filled:
  - #ownership   : full-bleed background slot, 16:9, ink overlay 72%
  - #cost        : full-bleed background slot, 21:9, ink overlay 78%
  - CommercePaths card 2 : inline slot, 4:3
  - TeamSection  : 1:1 portrait slots, 3 up
Do NOT add any placeholder people, silhouettes, or generated faces.

ACCEPTANCE
1. No `opacity` on [data-panel] or [data-tick] anywhere.
2. Inactive panel text >= 4.5:1 against its own panel; panel border >= 3:1.
3. At 1920x1080 and 1440x900 the composition fills the pinned pane; no gap
   above or below the content larger than the H2's own bottom margin.
4. Rail and skip link both visible at the first frame of the pin.
5. JS disabled: all four states present and readable.
6. prefers-reduced-motion: all four states stacked and readable, no animation.
7. 375px: carousel snaps, four dots track, no horizontal overflow.
8. No new hardcoded colour literals; everything routes through the tokens.
9. Lighthouse CLS on the homepage does not regress.
Order to apply them

Fix 1 first and look at it — that alone will change how the section reads, because three of the four panels come back. Then Fix 2, which is the bigger structural edit. Fixes 3–7 are twenty minutes together. Fix 8 is independent and can be done any time.

The two rules that do not bend

No faces you did not photograph. No stock people, no AI-generated portraits, not even as ambient background. Your best-qualified buyer has been burned by operators whose sites look exactly like that, and the association cannot be undone.

No screens showing numbers. A laptop with a rising graph, a phone with a sales figure, a dashboard with revenue — all are implied earnings claims and all are banned by your own build spec, in visuals as firmly as in copy.
