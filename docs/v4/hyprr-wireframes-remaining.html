The wholesale page carries the most weight. Read this first.

Three things have to be true on that page or the whole offer reads like an automation pitch:

  • Authorised distributors only, stated as a rule you operate under rather than a promise. Brand approval, distributor terms, invoices in the client's name.
  • An honest timeline. First purchase order around day 60–90, volume later. Publishing the slow part is what separates you from the people who hide it.

How wholesale actually works — the opportunity

The explainer the SERP demands, and the section that makes the business legible to an investor who has never sold on Amazon.

Every brand sells its products through more than one route. Some sell direct to Amazon. Some sell through distributors. Most also sell on their own website. Those routes do not cover every listing, every region or every moment of demand, and that gap is where a wholesale seller operates: buying genuine stock through an approved channel and selling it where the brand is not already serving the customer well. Diagram: Brand → direct · distributor · own site → the gap → your account.
